## Context

What is the issue we're seeing that motivates this decision? State the
forces at play — technical, business, organizational — as neutrally as
possible. Link the spike, issue, or discussion that produced the decision
where one exists.

## Decision

What is the change we're actually making? State it in full sentences,
active voice: "We will ...".

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder as a result of this decision? Include the
tradeoffs that were accepted, not just the benefits — a decision record
that only lists upsides isn't useful when someone later has to decide
whether to revisit it.
